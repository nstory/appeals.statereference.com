What is a public records appeal?

Under the Massachusetts public records law, if a requestor is unsatisfied with the response they received, they may open an appeal with the Supervisor of Records. The Supervisor will then make a determination, and may order an agency to provide an additional response.

These determinations are a rich resource providing insights into the public records law and its exemptions.

What is this database?

We download the determinations made by the Supervisor of Records from 2020 to present. We index those determinations to make them searchable online.

Why is this useful?

A few ideas:

1. Are you thinking of making a public records request, but you don't know what specifically to ask for?
Find requests made by previous requesters, often professional journalists.

2. Did a records access officer (RAO) deny your request?
Search the database to find similar requests in the past. See if the denials held-up when put under scrutiny.

How is this different from the state's database?

The Secretary of the Commonwealth provides the AppealsWeb interface for searching these appeals. This is a useful resource for searching particular fields and for accessing the older appeals we currently do not index.

The primary benefit of StateReference Appeals is that we provide full text search. Your search query is against all the text of the determination.
