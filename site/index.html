What is a public records appeal?

Under the Massachusetts public records law, a requester unsatisfied with an agency's response may appeal to the Supervisor of Records. The Supervisor then issues an order and may order the agency to respond further.

These determinations are a rich resource providing insights into the public records law and its exemptions.

What is this database?

We download the determinations made by the Supervisor of Records from 2020 to present and index them so you can search them online.

Why is this useful?

A few ideas:

1. Are you thinking of making a public records request, but you don't know what specifically to ask for?
Find requests made by previous requesters, often professional journalists.

2. Did a records access officer (RAO) deny your request?
Search the database for similar requests, and see whether those denials held up under scrutiny.

How is this different from the state's database?

The Secretary of the Commonwealth provides the AppealsWeb interface. It's useful for searching particular fields, and for accessing the older appeals we do not index.

The main advantage of StateReference Appeals is full-text search: your query is matched against the entire text of the determinations.
